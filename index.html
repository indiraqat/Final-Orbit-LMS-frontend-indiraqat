The Process

Up and running in minutes

Simple for HR to set up, intuitive for interns to navigate.

1

Admin creates onboarding tracks

HR builds structured course modules with materials, assignments, and due dates — tailored per role or department.

2

Interns enroll and start learning

New hires access their assigned tracks, work through modules at their own pace, and submit assignments directly on the platform.

3

Mentors review and give feedback

Supervisors get notified on submissions, leave feedback inline, and monitor completion progress without any back-and-forth emails.

4

Everyone tracks progress in real time

Interns see their progress bar and pending tasks. Admins see team-wide completion stats — all on a clean, role-specific dashboard.
