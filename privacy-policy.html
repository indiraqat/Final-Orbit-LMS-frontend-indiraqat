Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This is a placeholder Privacy Policy for Orbit LMS, a student coursework project. Orbit LMS is not a real company or product — this page exists to round out the site's footer links and does not describe the handling of any real personal data.

1. Information We Collect

In a real deployment, Orbit LMS would collect information such as:

  • Account details you provide when registering, like your name, work email, and department.
  • Learning activity, such as which materials you've viewed and your progress through each module.
  • Quiz results, including your score and answers for each mini quiz you complete.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected would be used to track onboarding progress, unlock the next material or quiz in a module, and let mentors and admins see which courses their team has taken. Quiz results are shown to you inside the app so you can print or save them yourself — Orbit LMS does not automatically send quiz results to your mentor on your behalf.

3. Data Sharing

We would not sell your information to third parties. Your mentor or admin can see your enrolled courses and completion status as part of managing your onboarding track.

4. Data Retention

Account and progress data would be kept for as long as your account is active, and removed within a reasonable period after account deactivation.

5. Cookies

Orbit LMS would use minimal cookies or local storage only to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences — not for advertising or cross-site tracking.

6. Your Rights

You would be able to request a copy of your data, ask for corrections, or request account deletion by contacting your administrator.

7. Changes to This Policy

This placeholder policy may be updated as the project develops. Check back here for the latest version.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this placeholder policy can be directed to the Orbit LMS project owner.

Note: this page is fictional content created for a student project and does not reflect a real privacy policy or real data practices.
