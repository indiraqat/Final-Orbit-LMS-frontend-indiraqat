About

Onboarding shouldn't feel like guesswork.

Orbit LMS is a coursework project — a made-up onboarding platform built to explore what a clean, structured internship and new-hire experience could look like: materials to learn from, a quiz per module to check understanding, and a simple way for interns and mentors to see where things stand.

Why we built this

New interns and employees often get onboarded through a scattered mix of documents, spreadsheets, and one-off messages. Orbit LMS imagines a single place instead: modules broken into materials and mini quizzes, with each module unlocking once the one before it is done.

Since this is a fictional project, none of the courses, companies, or people referenced anywhere on this site are real.

How it works, in short

1

Learn

Interns work through course materials at their own pace.

2

Quiz

Each module ends with a short quiz to check understanding.

3

Share

Interns print or save their quiz results and share them with their mentor.

A student project

Orbit LMS was built as a weekly coursework checkpoint, focused on front-end structure and interaction — global design tokens, reusable components, and static page layouts, without a backend behind it yet.
